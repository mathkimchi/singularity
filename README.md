# Sonamu

> Organize everything.

Sonamu let's you organize all your apps and their elements into one large hierarchy.

## The Problem

(Warning: though I tried to restrict this entire RRADME to facts and objective observations, I do assert some of my opinions. I trust the reader to think for themselves.)

In the standard modern desktop UI, your workspace is organized into virtual desktops and then windows and then whatever organizer each app uses. This window-based organizer comes with a few limitations:

For each tab within the app (eg: browser tab, editor tab, or the entire app for simple apps), organization is limited to within the app window, preventing you from forming connections across tabs and requiring you to use a new UI to traverse each app. This can be solved via a unified organization protocol.

Window-level organization replicates physical space, but it can quickly get cluttered just like a small desk with too many things. Most apps just store tabs in a list, which is hardly any organization. Neither system was intended to support connections between items.
In contrast, a tree-based hierarchicy is born from each node's relationship to other nodes, which reflects how people naturally think and organize ideas.
It also enables you to traverse at different depths, which enables speed and precision.

## Who is Sonamu for?

I am building Sonamu for personal use, but conceptually, I believe a standardized hierarchical organizer is better than the current status quo of the window-based paradigm for anyone who has many projects and apps open at a time.
